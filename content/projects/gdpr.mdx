# GDPR — Settings and Data Transparency

## Context
Meta's internal compliance tools needed better UX for managing user‑data
settings to align with GDPR standards.

## Approach
Our design system introduced clearer, hierarchical controls and contextual
education patterns to explain each choice.

![GDPR settings UI](/images/gdpr.png)

## Lessons
Simpler language and logical grouping drove a measurable uplift in
comprehension scores during testing.
