# Ads — Design System Revamp

## Problem
Ad creation workflows were fragmented across multiple internal tools,
making it difficult for teams to maintain consistency and scale new features.

## Goals
- Unify ad‑setup experience  
- Reduce creation time  
- Improve internal design handoffs  

## Process
We audited existing UIs and user feedback,
then rebuilt the flows using a shared component library.

![Wireframe of ad setup](/images/ads-screen.png)

## Impact
- Setup time reduced by ~30%  
- Fewer usability issues reported in feedback sessions  
- Higher adoption across cross‑functional teams
